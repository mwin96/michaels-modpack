Orbital Railgun Reforged (orbital_railgun 1.4.2, CurseForge file 7292536)
Authors: Mishkis, Forge port by TysonTheEmber
Source: https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/orbital-railgun-reforged
The 1.1 intro animation is made from the gallery images of the original Orbital Railgun project by Mishkis
(https://modrinth.com/mod/orbital-railgun), which is also MIT licensed.
Michael's Modpack changes the bundled crafting recipe in this jar. A datapack override loses to the mod's own recipe on
existing worlds, because a newly added mod is appended to the end of the world's datapack order.
